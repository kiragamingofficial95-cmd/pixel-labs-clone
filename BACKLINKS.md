# Pixel Labs — backlink plan (started 18 Nov 2026)

Reality: 1 external link (scamadviser.com, auto-generated). Zero useful links today.
Links matter most once there is something worth linking to — the `/work` page (week 4)
is that asset. Until then, only items 1–3 below make sense.

## Do now (week 4–6, ~1 hr each)

1. **Agora Reliance link** — the first real link, earned. Ask Agora Reliance to add
   "Website by Pixel Labs" (linked) in their footer credit. Client-site credits are
   standard practice. Owner: you. Done when the link is live.
2. **LinkedIn company page** — create "Pixel Labs" company page, complete every field
   (tagline, services, website, logo). Gives AI engines a consistent brand entity and
   becomes the `sameAs` profile for schema. Owner: you. Done when the page is live.
3. **X/Instagram profiles** — same setup, same purpose: consistent name, bio, and link.

## Do next (month 2, ~2 hrs each)

4. **Agency directories** — free listings: Clutch, DesignRush, GoodFirms, Sortlist.
   These rank for "web design agency" searches and get crawled by AI answers. Fill
   profiles with the same description as the homepage. Expect slow drip referral
   traffic, not rankings.
5. **Expert quotes (journalist platforms)** — sign up on Qwoted / Featured /
   Help a B2B Writer. Respond to queries about influencer marketing, creator
   economy, and agency websites as the niche expert. 1–2 placed quotes = real,
   editorial links from marketing publications. Owner: you (they quote a real
   person, which is the point).
6. **Podcast/newsletter pitches** — 5 pitches to influencer-marketing newsletters
   and podcasts: "what makes an agency website convert brands into campaigns".
   Pitch the `/work` page as proof.

## Do when content exists (month 3+)

7. **Honest community participation** — answer real questions in influencer
   marketing communities (Reddit r/influencermarketing, industry Slack/Discord
   groups) with disclosed affiliation, linking only where it genuinely helps
   (→ `reddit-quora-answers` skill).
8. **Partner listings** — influencer-tech SaaS partner pages (creator CRMs,
   analytics tools): if any tool you work with has a partner/agency directory,
   apply.
9. **Best-of-list placement** — once `/work` has 2–3 case studies, pitch the
   "best websites for influencer marketing agencies" listicles that AI answers
   cite (→ `best-of-list-inclusion` skill).

## Do not

- No bought links, link farms, PBNs, or "1,000 directories" blasts.
- No mass guest-post purchases (vet every sponsored placement first →
  `sponsored-placement-vetting`).
- No exchanging links with random sites.

## Measurement

- External links: Search Console → Links → External links (baseline: 1, 18 Nov 2026).
- Referring domains in any free backlink checker monthly.
- Goal by week 13: 5–10 referring domains, all legitimate.
